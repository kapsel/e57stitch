Loading scans
Only changes what you see, not the result. Saved with this project, for everyone using it, and reloads its scans.
Advanced
The benchmarks use a score of 0.15. Changing it can change the result, and stored results are then not reused.
Settings changed. Press Load scans to use them.
Removed points
Press Remove points, then drag a box in the top or side view. It applies to the selected scans, or to all scans if none are selected. The scan files are not changed: points are hidden in the preview and left out of exports.
Clip box
Keeps only the points inside the box. The scan files are not changed: points are hidden in the preview and left out of point and mesh exports. Registration is not affected. Empty X/Y means no limit.
Gravity check
A scan whose tilt is uncertain can be matched at any angle. Set a scan to level if it stood on a flat surface, or free to stop assuming which way is up.
